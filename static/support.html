Getting started

  1. Make an account at katchjobs.online, then sign in to the extension from its popup. The extension stores no personal details of its own — everything it knows comes from your account.
  2. Upload a resume. Open the extension popup, expand Upload a resume, and give it a PDF. It is read once and turned into a structured profile. Everything else — match scores, drafted answers — reads that profile.
  3. Fill in your application answers on the dashboard, under Application answers. These are the questions every form asks: notice period, work authorisation, availability. Answered once, they are suggested from then on, and they cost nothing to reuse.

Common problems

"No job posting detected on this page"

The extension reads job pages on the sites listed in its manifest. On any other site, open the popup and click Enable Copilot on this site, which grants access to that one site and nothing else. If the site is already enabled, reload the page — a tab opened before the permission was granted has no reader in it.

Some pages genuinely do not say who the employer is. When that happens the popup asks you rather than guessing, because a wrong company name splits one application into two rows that never reconcile.

The match card does not appear

It is shown only while you are signed in, and it has an off switch in the extension's Settings panel. Check both. The score it shows is plain keyword matching done on your own server — no AI call, and nothing is charged for it.

A drafted answer is empty

Questions asking for a fact about you — your current salary, your notice period, where you live — are deliberately never invented. If nothing is saved that answers one, the extension says which detail to fill in rather than producing a plausible wrong number that nobody checks before submitting. Add it under Application answers and it is filled in from then on.

An application I expected is not in my tracker

If you connected Gmail, open Activity on the dashboard and turn on Show mail the filter rejected before classifying it. A rule filter runs before anything else and drops bulk mail; every rejection is listed there with the rule that caused it. If a real message is in that list, email us the line and the rule gets fixed.

Sign-in fails, or the popup says the server is unreachable

Open the extension's Settings panel and check the API address. It should be https://katchjobs.online unless you are running your own server.

Your data

What is collected and why is set out in full in the privacy policy. In short: your data goes to your own account and nowhere else, resume text and job descriptions are processed by Google Gemini to produce scores and drafts, and you can export or delete everything from the dashboard's Settings tab at any time.

To delete your account and everything in it, use Settings → Delete account on the dashboard, or email the address above and ask.

Reporting a bug

Email anipy2000@gmail.com. What helps:

  • What you were doing, and what you expected instead.
  • The page you were on, if it is a public job posting.
  • The extension version, from chrome://extensions.
  • Anything in the browser console, if you are comfortable opening it.

Please do not include passwords or anything from a private application form. Nothing about your account is needed to look into a bug beyond the address you signed up with.
