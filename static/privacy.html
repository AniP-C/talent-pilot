Privacy Policy

Talent Pilot — job application tracker and browser extension

Last updated: 8 August 2026

Talent Pilot is a personal job-application tracker. It consists of a website at katchjobs.online and a Chrome extension, which share one account. This policy explains exactly what the service stores, why, and how to get rid of it.

Talent Pilot is operated by an individual developer, not a company. There is no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind. Your data is never sold, rented, or shared for marketing.

What is collected

DataWhere it comes fromWhy
Email address and password You, at sign-up To create and sign you into your account. Passwords are stored only as a PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 hash with a per-account salt — never in readable form.
Resume contents — name, email, phone, location, work history, education, skills The PDF you upload To score job descriptions against your background and to pre-fill your application answers.
Application answers — including salary expectations, notice period, work authorisation, and any equal-opportunity responses you choose to give (gender, ethnicity, veteran and disability status) You, in the questionnaire So the extension can offer them back to you when a form asks. Every one of these is optional; leave any blank and it is simply never suggested.
Job postings you save — company, role, job description, and the page URL The job page, when you click Save To build your application tracker.
Gmail message metadata and body text — sender, subject, and content of recruiting emails Your Gmail account, only if you connect it To work out which application an email is about and update its status.
IP address and sign-in attempts Your browser, at sign-in Rate limiting, to stop password guessing. Not used for anything else.

What the extension stores in your browser

Only four things, all in Chrome's local extension storage: your sign-in token, your email address, the server address you are pointed at, and the name of your selected resume profile. Answers you generate with AI are also cached locally so the same question is not paid for twice. Your saved answers and tracked applications are not stored in the extension — they live in your account.

What is never collected

  • Your browsing history. The extension reads a page only on the job sites listed in its manifest, or on a site you have explicitly granted it access to.
  • Keystrokes, mouse movement, scrolling, or any behavioural analytics.
  • Payment or card details. The service is free and has no payment processing.
  • Anything from a page you have not asked the extension to work on.

Google user data and the Gmail connection

Connecting Gmail is entirely optional; the rest of Talent Pilot works without it. If you connect it, Talent Pilot requests a single scope:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly

This grants read-only access. Talent Pilot cannot send, delete, or modify any message. It searches only recent mail matching recruiting-related terms, reads the sender, subject and body text of those messages, and uses them to update the status of applications you are already tracking.

Limited Use disclosure. Talent Pilot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Specifically: Google user data is used only to provide and improve the application-tracking features you can see in the app; it is never sold; it is never transferred to others except as needed to provide those features, for security purposes, or to comply with applicable law; and it is never used for advertising. No human reads your Google user data except where you have given explicit consent, for security purposes, or to comply with applicable law.

Your Gmail authorisation token is stored in your own workspace on the server and is used only to make requests on your behalf. You can disconnect Gmail at any time from the dashboard sidebar, which deletes that token. You can also revoke access from your Google Account permissions page.

AI processing

Talent Pilot uses Google's Gemini API for four things: turning your uploaded resume into structured data, comparing a job description against it, drafting answers to application questions, and classifying recruiting emails.

The relevant text is sent to Google for processing and the result is returned. Requests are made under a paid-tier-eligible developer API key. Google does not use data submitted through the Gemini API to train its models, per the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service. Talent Pilot sends only what is needed for each task and never transmits your password.

Where your data is stored

On a single virtual machine hosted with Google Cloud Platform in the United States. Every account has its own isolated database file, keyed by an internal account number. Traffic is encrypted in transit with HTTPS (TLS certificates from Let's Encrypt). Backups are taken nightly and kept for 14 days.

Sign-in tokens are stored as SHA-256 digests, so a copy of the database does not grant anyone a live session. Changing your password revokes every existing session.

Sharing

Your data is shared with exactly three parties, all of them infrastructure:

  • Google Cloud Platform — hosts the server the service runs on.
  • Google Gemini API — performs the AI processing described above.
  • Google Gmail API — only if you connect Gmail.

There are no other recipients. Data is never sold, never rented, never used to determine creditworthiness or for lending, and never used for any purpose unrelated to running Talent Pilot.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. You can remove most of it yourself at any time: delete individual applications and resume profiles from the dashboard, clear any saved answer from the Application answers tab, and disconnect Gmail from the sidebar.

To delete your account and everything in it, email the address below. Everything is removed within 30 days, including from backups as they age out of the 14-day window. Uninstalling the extension removes what it kept in your browser but does not delete your account.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Talent Pilot honours all of these regardless of jurisdiction — email the address below and it will be handled.

Children

Talent Pilot is not intended for anyone under 16, and data is not knowingly collected from them.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top will be updated and a notice will be shown in the dashboard. Continuing to use the service after that means accepting the revised policy.
